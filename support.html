Help & Support

Need help with Epic Roots: Chowka Bara & More? We're here to help you enjoy your gaming experience.

How Can We Help?

Choose the topic closest to your issue. You can contact our support email for assistance with any of these areas.

Accounts

Account & Sign-In

Need help accessing your game account, Google sign-in, guest profile, account linking or recovery?

Describe your issue and include your game display name or Account ID, if available.

Multiplayer

Online Games & Friends

Experiencing problems with matchmaking, friend invitations, disconnects or multiplayer gameplay?

Tell us which board, game mode and player count you were using.

Gameplay

Rules & Game Issues

Found an unexpected game result, movement issue, visual problem or other gameplay bug?

Explain what happened, what you expected and how to reproduce it.

Virtual Economy

Coins, Gems & Collections

Have questions about virtual currency balances, rewards, cosmetics or owned items?

Include the relevant game screen and approximate time of the issue.

Payments

Purchases & Transactions

Where purchases are available, contact us about missing items, purchase verification or transaction-related concerns.

Do not send payment-card numbers or other financial credentials.

Privacy

Privacy & Account Deletion

Need information about your personal data or want to request deletion of your Epic Roots account?

Contact us with the subject "Epic Roots - Privacy Request" or "Epic Roots - Account Deletion".

Before Contacting Support

The following information can help us investigate your request:

  • Your game display name or Account ID, if available
  • Your device model and Android version
  • The installed game version, if known
  • A brief description of the problem
  • The approximate date and time it occurred
  • Screenshots when relevant and safe to share

Important: Never share your password, one-time verification codes, authentication tokens, payment-card details or other sensitive secrets.

Please contact support through the official address listed below rather than sharing private account details publicly.
