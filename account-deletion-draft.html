Account Deletion

Request deletion of your account and associated personal data for Epic Roots: Chowka Bara & More, an Android game developed by GameAJS.

Pre-publication draft: This page is being prepared for the game's release. The live account-deletion process is undergoing verification.

1. How to Request Account Deletion

You can submit an account-deletion request by contacting GameAJS support. You do not need to reinstall the game to contact us.

Request deletion by email

Send an email with the subject: Epic Roots - Account Deletion Request

Please include your game display name or Account ID, if available. If possible, contact us from the email associated with your linked account.

Request Account Deletion

Email: epicroots.gameajs@gmail.com

If you use a guest account or cannot access your linked email, describe your situation in your request so support can explain the available account-verification process.

Never send your password, authentication tokens, payment card details, or other sensitive credentials.

2. Account Verification

GameAJS may request additional information to confirm that the account belongs to you. This protects accounts from unauthorized deletion requests.

Sending an email starts the support request process. It does not automatically delete your account.

3. What Account Deletion Covers

Account deletion is intended to remove or anonymize the account and associated personal data, except for information that must legitimately be retained.

Depending on the account, associated information may include:

  • Game account and profile information
  • Linked authentication information
  • Friends and social account associations
  • Gameplay progress, statistics and achievements
  • Player-specific game settings
  • Virtual wallet and cosmetic ownership records
  • Player-associated multiplayer records

Deleting your account may permanently remove access to game progress and virtual items associated with that account.

4. Information That May Be Retained

Limited information may need to be retained for legitimate purposes, including:

  • Compliance with applicable legal obligations
  • Purchase, refund or payment disputes
  • Fraud prevention and account security
  • Required financial or transaction records
  • Outstanding legal holds or investigations
  • Minimal records necessary to document deletion

Any retained information should be limited to what is necessary for the applicable purpose and retained only for the relevant required period.

The precise retention categories, exceptions and periods must be confirmed against GameAJS's operational retention policy before this draft is finalized.

5. Deletion Processing and Cancellation

Once your request and account ownership have been reviewed, GameAJS support will provide information about the applicable deletion process and status.

Any applicable pending-deletion period, cancellation options and completion timeline will be explained during the verified request process.
