Privacy Policy

Draft prepared: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how GameAJS handles information in connection with Epic Roots: Chowka Bara & More, our Android game.

It explains what information may be processed when players use game accounts, multiplayer services, optional purchases, advertising features, and support.

1. Information Processed

Depending on the features used and enabled in the installed game version, the following categories of information may be processed.

  • Account information: game account identifiers, player names, selected profile pictures, and authentication information. When Google sign-in is used, account details provided through the authentication process may also be processed.
  • Gameplay information: match participation, gameplay results, statistics, achievements, player progress, and game settings.
  • Social and multiplayer information: friends, match invitations, matchmaking activity, and predefined in-game emote interactions.
  • Virtual economy information: Coins, Gems, virtual item ownership, rewards, and relevant in-game transaction records.
  • Purchase information: where real-money purchases are available, transaction identifiers and purchase verification information may be processed to deliver purchased digital goods.
  • Technical information: information necessary for server connectivity, troubleshooting, security and reliability, which may include IP addresses, device or app details, error information, and service logs.
  • Support information: information voluntarily provided when contacting GameAJS for assistance.

2. How Information Is Used

Information processed by the game is used, as applicable, to:

  • Authenticate players and maintain game accounts.
  • Provide online matchmaking and multiplayer gameplay.
  • Save achievements, progress, statistics and settings.
  • Maintain virtual wallets, purchases and item ownership.
  • Help prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorized activity.
  • Investigate technical problems and improve reliability.
  • Respond to support and privacy inquiries.
  • Meet applicable legal and platform obligations.

3. Information Visible to Other Players

During multiplayer gameplay, other players may see your game display name, selected avatar, gameplay actions, match status, results, and predefined emotes.

Epic Roots does not provide free-form player text messaging or voice chat as part of its documented gameplay design.

The game is not designed to share your precise physical location with other players.

4. Third-Party Services

Some game features rely on external services. Depending on which features are included and active, these may involve:

  • Google authentication services: for Google account sign-in and identity verification.
  • Google Play services: for platform functionality and, where enabled, in-app purchase processing and verification.
  • Google AdMob: where advertising is enabled, advertising services may process device identifiers, ad interaction information, and related technical information according to their applicable practices.
  • Infrastructure providers: hosting and network services may process information necessary to operate and secure online gameplay.

For information about Google's privacy practices, visit Google's Privacy Policy .

5. Advertising

Epic Roots may offer optional rewarded advertisements when those features are enabled.

Advertising providers may process technical information and advertising identifiers as applicable to the installed version, device settings, consent choices, and regional requirements.

Any advertising and consent features must be configured in accordance with applicable privacy requirements before they are activated.

6. In-App Purchases

If real-money digital purchases are offered, payment processing is handled through the applicable Google Play purchase system.

GameAJS may process purchase tokens, transaction identifiers, purchase status, and entitlement information for verification, fulfillment, account recovery, and refund handling.

7. Data Security

GameAJS aims to protect player information using security measures appropriate to the game services, including access restrictions, account verification, and protected network communications.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Players should avoid sharing account credentials or sensitive personal information with others.

8. Data Retention

Account and gameplay records may be retained while required to operate the game account, maintain game progress, provide support, or fulfill legitimate security and legal needs.

Certain transaction, security, or audit records may require different retention treatment. Applicable retention periods and deletion exceptions will depend on the final operational data policies.

9. Account and Data Deletion

Players may contact GameAJS to request deletion of their Epic Roots account and associated data.

To initiate an inquiry or deletion request, email epicroots.gameajs@gmail.com .

Account ownership may need to be verified before a deletion request can be processed. Do not send passwords or authentication secrets.

Where information must be retained for legitimate security, fraud prevention, or legal purposes, that information will be handled under the applicable retention policy.

10. Children's Privacy

Epic Roots is based on traditional board-game experiences. The game's applicable age classification and intended audience are determined through its Google Play declarations.

Parents or guardians with questions about a child's information may contact GameAJS using the address provided below.

11. Changes to This Policy

GameAJS may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in game functionality, service providers, legal requirements, or data-handling practices.

The updated policy will be made available through this webpage with an updated revision date.
